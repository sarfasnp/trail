Al Raya Hotel Apartment – Al-Bassam Petroleum Equipment Company

Al Raya Hotel Apartment is a standard Apartment style & centrally located in the heart of Bur Dubai. walking distance from Burujman shopping Centre & spines Centre, it is only 10 minutes from Dubai International Airport, the Jumeirah Beach, Dubai Down Town and Burj Khalifa. Al Raya Hotel Apartments offers very species 2 & 3 bedrooms Apartments with high speed internet access offered free of cost, swimming pool, Sauna, Steam & Gymnasium.
